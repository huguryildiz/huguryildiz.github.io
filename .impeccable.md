## Design Context

### Users

Academic peers, program committees, funding reviewers, prospective graduate students, and
other visitors assessing Hüseyin Uğur Yıldız's scholarly record. They need to understand the
research program, verify publications and professional activity, and explore technical work
without mistaking simulated or exploratory material for verified evidence.

### Brand Personality

Sober, scientific, and operational. The interface should convey confidence through restraint,
clarity, factual precision, and professional presentation.

### Aesthetic Direction

An editorial academic record with a restrained scientific-instrument character. Use the existing
STIX Two Text and Source Sans 3 typography, map-like visual language, warm rust accent, and
ink-tinted light and dark themes. Avoid cartoon, arcade, neon, sci-fi HUD, generic dashboard,
glassmorphism, and decorative-motion aesthetics.

### Design Principles

1. Put scholarly accuracy and operational meaning ahead of visual spectacle.
2. Preserve a clear reading hierarchy and adapt critical interactions across desktop and mobile.
3. Use existing tokens, typography, semantic colors, and component patterns before adding new ones.
4. Keep simulated, exploratory, and publication-backed material visibly distinct and honestly labeled.
5. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and cues
   beyond color alone.

### Current Scope: Scroll Enhancement

The user clarified that the requested work is fundamentally the scroll experience.
Preserve the incumbent typography, colors, navigation, portrait, content order, metrics,
compact selected-publication lists, software list, and other academic page layouts.
Do not apply the broader editorial redesign. Identity remains directly on the ocean;
an opaque left identity card was explicitly rejected.

Add a short native, reversible camera sequence and a skip link. Presentation progress
must remain independent of simulation time. Explore owns the camera until exited.
Keep mobile, reduced-motion, enlarged-text and fallback views usable without pinning.
