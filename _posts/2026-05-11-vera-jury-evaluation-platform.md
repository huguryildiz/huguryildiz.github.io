It's everything that happens after. Three hours of evaluation can turn into days of Excel merging, score normalization, and accreditation paperwork.

The evaluation should be the work. The paperwork shouldn't.

Meet **VERA**, a jury evaluation platform that turns post-jury work into a structured, export-ready workflow.

- **Jurors** scan a QR code, enter a PIN, and score from their phone. No accounts, no app to install.
- **Coordinators** monitor live rankings and jury activity in real time.
- **When the jury ends**, ABET / MÜDEK criterion-mapped reports, XLSX/PDF exports, and a tamper-evident audit trail are ready.

VERA debuts at TED University's Electrical and Electronics Engineering Capstone Exhibition on May 22.
