Algae communicate through chemicals, voltage, and light. The **Internet of Algae Lab** translates communication-theoretic models for photosynthetic molecular networks into parameter-driven simulations that run in a browser.

The lab covers four signaling modalities:

- **Quorum sensing:** chemical communication between cells
- **Volatile organic compounds (VOCs):** airborne chemical signals
- **Electrical signaling:** communication through voltage changes
- **Bioluminescence:** communication through emitted light

Users can modify model parameters, visualize signal propagation toward the receiver, and examine attenuation and propagation delay as functions of distance. The lab is a static web page and requires no installation or build step.

The simulations translate models from *The Internet of Algae: End-to-End Communication-Theoretic Models for Photosynthetic Molecular Networks*, by Ian F. Akyildiz, Selin Sayın, Eyüp İlker Saygılı, and Bige Deniz Ünlütürk, published in **IEEE Transactions on Molecular, Biological, and Multi-Scale Communications**.

These are communication-theoretic simulations, not experimental validation of biological signaling. The project is available here:

- [Try the live lab →](https://internet-of-algae-lab.vercel.app)
- [Read the code →](https://github.com/huguryildiz/internet-of-algae-lab)
