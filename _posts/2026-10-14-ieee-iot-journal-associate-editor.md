I have joined the editorial board of the **IEEE Internet of Things Journal** as an Associate Editor.

<figure class="post-figure">
  <img src="/assets/images/writing/ieee-iotj-associate-editor.webp" alt="Announcement graphic reading Associate Editor, IEEE Internet of Things Journal, with the journal's cover on the left and a glowing globe connected to IoT device icons on the right." loading="lazy" width="1200" height="1500">
</figure>

We welcome submissions on IoT communication and networking, particularly terrestrial and underwater sensor networks, UAV networks, and machine learning for combinatorial optimization (ML4CO) applied to networking problems.

I thank Dr. Tiago Koketsu Rodrigues, Associate Editor-in-Chief, for the nomination and the editorial board for their trust.
