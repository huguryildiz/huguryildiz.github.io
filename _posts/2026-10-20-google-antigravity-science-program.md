I have been selected for the **Google Antigravity science program**, which gives up to 10,000 researchers six months of access to **Gemini 4 Argon**.

It will support three of my ongoing research collaborations:

1. **Underwater IoT for seismic monitoring**, with Sefa Geleri (MSc student) and Erim Ergün (undergraduate student) at TED University. We are evaluating machine learning for combinatorial optimization (ML4CO) methods for sensor placement and reliable routing.
2. **Entanglement scheduling in underwater quantum networks**, with Prof. Elif Tuğçe Ceran (Middle East Technical University), studying entanglement freshness under quantum memory, delivery-rate and resource constraints.
3. **[Internet of Algae](/writing/internet-of-algae-lab/)**, with Prof. Ian F. Akyildiz (Algaentia BioTech Labs), Prof. Bige Deniz Unluturk (Michigan State University) and Prof. Eyüp İlker Saygılı (Sanko University), simulating communication channel models in algae as a foundation for future digital twin development.

Across these projects, I plan to use Gemini 4 Argon through Antigravity to review the literature, build source-grounded RAG workflows, develop optimization and machine learning models, explore digital twin modeling of molecular communication channels, and automate reproducible experiments.
